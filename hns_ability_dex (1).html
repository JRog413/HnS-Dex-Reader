HnS Ability Dex

Pokémon Heart & Soul

In HnS 1.2.1, two separate systems can change abilities: a fixed Legendary Ability Upgrade table (swaps ~9 legendaries off Pressure onto a signature ability, e.g. Ho-oh → Serene Grace) and a full Random Abilities mode that swaps every species' ability set with another species, seeded from your save's trainer ID. Toggle the settings below to match what you enabled at the start of your save.

Legendary Ability Upgrade (affects ~9 legendaries + Noctowl)
Advanced: Full Random Abilities mode (only if you enabled this at New Game)

Random Abilities is seeded from your save's Trainer ID. Upload your .sav below so it can be read (Chaos Mode isn't supported — it uses a live, non-seeded RNG in-game and can't be predicted from a save file).
